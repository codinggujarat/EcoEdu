Frequently Asked Questions

Is EcoEdu free for schools?
Yes! The core platform is 100% free for educational institutions, teachers, and students to use. Our goal is to democratize environmental education.
How are challenges verified?
Students submit photo evidence and written reflections for their challenges. These are securely routed to their connected teacher's Dashboard for 1-click approval or feedback.
Is student data kept private?
Absolutely. We comply with all major student data privacy laws. Profiles are only visible within closed school networks, and student photos are strictly moderated.
